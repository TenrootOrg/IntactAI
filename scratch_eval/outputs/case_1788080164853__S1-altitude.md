## Assessment

Nine hosts are implicated across 757 days (2024-05-24 to 2026-06-21), with credential-access tooling, encoded PowerShell, security-control impairment, suspicious services and lateral-access indicators dominating. The evidence peaks in a coordinated 2026-06-16–21 cluster spanning endpoints, both domain controllers, the certificate authority and software-distribution server; however, the 25-month spread and repeated security-testing tools support **several episodes or authorised exercises**, not one continuous campaign. Overall confidence: **MODERATE** — activity is demonstrably high risk, but operator identity, change records and source/destination telemetry are absent.

- **Zoom** — `ALClient022` from 2026-06-16T06:30:00Z to 2026-06-18T09:00:00Z, then all nine hosts from 2026-06-20T02:00:00Z to 2026-06-21T14:00:00Z.
